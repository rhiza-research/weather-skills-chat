# Invitations

Admins invite people by email, either to the platform or to one organization. Invitations need the mail settings on [[Built-in Tools]]. Until those are set, invitations cannot be sent.

## Kinds of invitation

| Kind | Who sends it | Role | Spending limit |
|-|-|-|-|
| Platform | A platform admin | User, or admin to make the person a platform admin | Set on the person's personal organization; 300 US dollars a month unless the admin chooses another amount or no limit |
| Organization | An admin of a workspace or platform organization | User or admin; always admin for the platform organization | Set on the person's membership; none unless the admin chooses one, and never above the organization's own limit |

A platform invitation is only for someone without an account. An organization invitation is only for someone who is not already a member. Personal organizations cannot be invited to. Spending limits are described on [[Usage and Spending Caps]].

## The invitation email

The email links to the app's invitation page, using the public address set on [[Deployment]]. The link works once and expires after a week. Admins see outstanding invitations and can resend or cancel them. Resending, or inviting the same address again, sends a new link and makes earlier links stop working. If the email cannot be sent, the invitation is still saved, and the admin can resend it.

## Accepting

The person opens the link and accepts.

- A person without an account chooses a name and password, and the account is created.
- A person with an account must be signed in to that account. Someone signed in as a different person cannot accept it.
- An organization invitation adds the person to the organization with the invited role. A platform admin invitation makes the person a platform admin. A platform user invitation activates an account that is waiting for approval.

Accepting applies the spending limit and signs the person in. A used, expired, or canceled invitation cannot be accepted.
