# Share Links

A share link gives a read-only view of a chat as it was when the link was made: its messages and a copy of its files. Anyone signed in to the app who has the link can open it until it expires. On the shared page they can read the messages, browse, preview, and download the files, and clone the chat to continue it. Where a clone goes is described on [[Organizations]].

## Creating a link

Only a chat's owner can share it. Choosing **Share** from the chat's menu opens a dialog with an expiry, in days, that defaults to 7 and can be set from 1 to 365. **Create share link** makes the link and copies it to the clipboard.

Each link is separate. Sharing the chat again makes a new link with the chat as it is then. Earlier links keep showing what they showed when they were made, each until its own expiry. Messages and files added to the chat after a link is made never reach that link.

## Expiry and removal

When a link expires it stops working, and the app deletes its copy of the chat and files within about an hour. Links made before share links had an expiry do not expire.

Deleting a chat deletes all of its links and their copies.

## Links in emails

Each email sent with the email tool makes a new link that expires after 7 days. The footer shows the link and its expiry date. See [[Built-in Tools]].
